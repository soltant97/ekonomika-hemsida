FAQ

Good to know

How does the digital workflow work?

We build a digital solution based on the system your bookkeeping is in today. Documents from your connected sources, such as receipt sources, company cards and letters, end up in one and the same receipt inbox. You then review reports, budget and tax returns.

Which businesses do you work with?

We help businesses in every industry and of every size, from tradespeople and hairdressers to doctors, lawyers, IT and consulting companies.

Which services can I hire you for?

Ongoing bookkeeping, invoicing, VAT reporting, payroll, year-end closing, annual reports (K2 and K3), tax returns and reporting. We also help with company formation and financial advisory.

How do I get started?

Fill in the form on the home page or the contact page and briefly tell us what your business needs. We get back to you and go through the setup together with you.
