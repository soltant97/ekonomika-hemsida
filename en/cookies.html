Cookies and browser storage

Short version: this website uses no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Cookies

This website uses no cookies, neither our own nor third-party. That is why we show no cookie banner.

Browser storage

We store no information in your browser (for example localStorage), and we use no web analytics, tracking or advertising.

Typeface from Google Fonts

The typeface is loaded from Google Fonts. Your IP address is then sent to Google, but we set no cookies for it. Read more in our privacy policy.

Links to other websites

The icons in the footer are ordinary links to Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn. Nothing from those services is loaded on our website. If you click through, their own terms and cookie policies apply.

If this changes

If we later start using cookies or similar technology that is not necessary for the website to work, such as web analytics, we will ask for your consent first, in line with Chapter 9, Section 28 of the Swedish Electronic Communications Act. This page will then be updated.
