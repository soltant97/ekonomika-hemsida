Important dates for your business

VAT, employer declarations, year-end and tax returns. See when everything is due, and we keep track of the rest.

Every monththe 12th

Employer declaration, deducted tax and employer contributions. F-tax is paid. VAT if you report monthly. In January and August the 17th applies.

  1. JanuaryNow

    • 17JanEmployer declaration and F-tax for December.Limited companySole trader
    • 31JanIncome statements, for example for dividends and interest.Limited companySole trader

  2. FebruaryNow

    • 12FebVAT for October–December if you report quarterly.Limited companySole trader
    • 12FebPay in residual tax above SEK 30,000 to avoid interest.Sole traderPersonal

  3. MarchNow

    • 12MarResidual tax if you received your final tax assessment in December.Sole traderPersonal
    • MarduringThe tax return opens for individuals and sole traders.Sole traderPersonal

  4. MayNow

    • MayearlyLast day for income tax return 1. Sole traders file the NE appendix and should have closed the books.Sole traderPersonal
    • MayearlyExtra payment if residual tax is at most SEK 30,000, to avoid interest.Sole traderPersonal
    • 12MayVAT for January–March if you report quarterly.Limited companySole trader

  5. JuneNow

    • 30JunLast day for the annual general meeting. The annual report must be ready two weeks before, six weeks if the company has an auditor.Limited company

  6. JulyNow

    • 31JulThe annual report must be filed with the Companies Registration Office, or a late fee applies.Limited company

  7. AugustNow

    • 1AugIncome tax return 2 for the limited company.Limited company
    • 17AugVAT for April–June if you report quarterly, and annual VAT for limited companies.Limited companySole trader

  8. NovemberNow

    • 12NovVAT for July–September if you report quarterly.Limited companySole trader
    • 12NovResidual tax if you received your final tax assessment in August.Sole traderPersonal

  9. DecemberNow

    • 1DecLast day to file a preliminary tax return if next year’s F-tax should be based on your own forecast.Limited companySole trader
    • 31DecThe financial year ends. Year-end closing begins.Limited companySole trader

Applies to companies whose financial year is the calendar year and whose turnover is at most SEK 40 million. If a date falls on a weekend or public holiday, it moves to the next weekday. Exact dates for your company are on the Swedish Tax Agency’s My pages – or just ask us.
