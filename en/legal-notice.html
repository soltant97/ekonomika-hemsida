Anti-money laundering and customer due diligence

As an accounting consultant we are covered by the Swedish act (2017:630) on measures against money laundering and terrorist financing, and are supervised by the County Administrative Board. This means we must find out who our clients are, understand their business and carry out a risk assessment. We may therefore ask for identity documents and information on, among other things, owners, business activity and the source of funds. If we do not receive the information the law requires, we may have to decline or end an assignment.
