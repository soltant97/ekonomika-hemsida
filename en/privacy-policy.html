Privacy policy

How Ekonomika DS AB processes your personal data under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Last updated: 17 November 2026

1. Who is responsible for your data?

Ekonomika DS AB (reg. no. 559606-0045) is the controller for the processing described here. You can reach us at ekonomikakontakt@gmail.com.

2. What data do we process, and why?

  • When you contact us (through the form or by email): your name, email address, what you need help with and whatever you write yourself. We use it to reply and prepare a proposal. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract, or our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Article 6(1)(b) and (f)).
  • If you become a client: the data needed for the assignment, such as contact and company details and the material you provide for bookkeeping, payroll or tax returns. Legal basis: contract and legal obligations such as the Swedish Bookkeeping Act and the anti-money-laundering act (Article 6(1)(b) and (c)). We give more information when the assignment starts.

3. The contact form

The form on this website sends your message through the Formspree service (Formspree Inc., USA), which receives it and forwards it to our email. Formspree processes the data on our behalf as a data processor. The transfer to the USA is based on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. You can also write to us directly by email.

4. What the website itself collects

We use no cookies, no web analytics and no advertising (see Cookies and storage). Two things are still worth knowing:

  • The typeface is hosted on our own website, so no information is sent to Google or any other service when a page loads.
  • The operator of the web server may technically log IP address and time of visit, as is common for all websites.

5. Who may receive the data?

We never sell personal data. Data may be handled by suppliers who help us run the business, for example an email provider and web host, who then act on our instructions. Processing sometimes takes place in the United States. The transfer then relies on the safeguards the GDPR requires, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses. Data is also disclosed to authorities when we are legally obliged to.

6. How long is the data kept?

Enquiries that do not lead to an assignment are normally deleted within 12 months. Client data is kept while the assignment runs and afterwards for as long as the law requires. For example, accounting records must normally be kept for seven years under the Swedish Bookkeeping Act, and customer due diligence records for five years under the anti-money-laundering act.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • know what data we hold about you and receive a copy,
  • have incorrect data corrected,
  • in some cases have data erased, restrict the processing or object to it,
  • in some cases receive your data in a usable format (data portability),
  • withdraw consent you have given.

Contact ekonomikakontakt@gmail.com and we will help you. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority, the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY), imy.se.

8. Automated decisions

We make no decisions based solely on automated processing, and we do not use profiling.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when something changes. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
